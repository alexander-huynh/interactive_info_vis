Context: This clock visualizes a 25-minute Pomodoro as a 5×5 grid of sticky notes. Each minute is one note; the active note fills smoothly with seconds so progress feels like checking off tasks instead of watching a countdown.

  • Context: Designed for students studying in a browser. Lives quietly in the corner of the page and provides ambient awareness without demanding attention.
  • Design decisions:
    • 5×5 grid maps exactly to 25 minutes -> no legend needed.
    • Status contrast: completed notes brighten, future notes dim; the active note has a thicker outline for instant focus.
    • Seconds are confined to a local fill animation inside the active note to keep motion calm and readable.
  • Future work: 1–2 ideas for improvements or extensions.
    • Hover tooltip with exact minute label; click to reset or enter break mode.
    • Possible accessibility pass (color-contrast tuning + optional text/icon labels for color-blind users).

Sketch narrative: Apples from Branch

Replace this entire block with your own text when submitting.

Context: Twenty-five apples hang from a branch; one drops each minute. The active apple gently sways to show seconds and fallen apples accumulate on the ground.

  • Context: Designed for students studying in a browser. Lives quietly in the corner of the page and provides ambient awareness without demanding attention.
  • Design decisions:
    • Branch + twigs instead of a clothesline to fit the apple metaphor.
    • Fallen apples render on the ground so completion is visible, not disappearing.
    • Subtle sway on the active apple to encode seconds.
  • Future work: 1–2 ideas for improvements or extensions.
    • Ground shadow/gradient; optional click-to-drop (demo only).
    • Alternate symbol/pattern for color-blind accessibility.
